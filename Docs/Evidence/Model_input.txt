Evidence ID: E3

Source: Synthetic music library and simulated listening histories.

Evidence type: Synthetic data.

**Music library:**
- 60 songs
- 20 artists
- 10 genres
- 20 songs assigned to the familiar music pool
- 40 songs assigned to the unfamiliar music pool

**Experimental conditions:**
- Baseline: 80% familiar music and 20% unfamiliar music.
- Alternative: 40% familiar music and 60% unfamiliar music.
- 20 songs per listening history.
- 30 simulations per condition.
- Random seeds ranging from 42 to 71.

**Relevance:** The synthetic data provides controlled inputs for examining the relationship between listening-history familiarity and recommendation diversity.

**Location:** The music library is defined in SpotifyPrototype.py. Generated listening histories are stored in outputs/baseline_history.csv and outputs/alternative_history.csv.

**Limitation:** The music library and familiarity classifications are artificially constructed for the experiment and do not represent actual Spotify listening data or user preferences.
